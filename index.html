❓ How to Play

Stats decay over real time

Hunger and Happiness each drop about 6 points per hour while your pet is awake. Energy drains slower, about 3.6 points per hour. This happens based on the clock, so it keeps going even if you close the tab — the game catches up when you come back.

Feed

Feeds from your inventory restore hunger (and sometimes energy) based on what you bought in the Shop. If you're out of food, you get a free small snack (+5 hunger) — but only twice per hour.

Play

Opens a menu with two mini-games to choose from: Catch the Bugs and Fire Duty. Both earn coins and happiness — pick either one whenever you hit Play.

Catch the Bugs

Click the beetles before they scurry off. You have 15 seconds — more catches means more coins and happiness.

Dance

Uses your camera to track motion. Really move — wave, jump, swing your arms — until the meter crosses the line and you settle for a beat. Do it 3 times to earn happiness and +5 enrichment (dancing costs a bit of energy too).

Fire Duty

Dumpster fires pop up randomly. Press and hold inside the play area to aim your hose truck's water stream at a fire, and keep it aimed until the fire's progress bar fills. Fires cool back down if you look away. 20-second rounds pay coins and happiness.

Chat

Uses your microphone. Talk to your pet — the timer only fills up while you're actually speaking above a quiet room. Reach a full minute of talking to earn happiness, coins, and +10 enrichment.

Enrichment

A slow-building level bar for your pet's overall enrichment, filled up by Dance and Chat. Once it hits 100 it levels up (Lv 2, Lv 3, ...) and keeps going from 0.

Sleep

Puts your pet to sleep so Energy regenerates quickly (about 20 points per hour) instead of draining. The whole screen switches to a dark theme while your pet sleeps.

Shop & Inventory

Spend coins on food, toys, room decor, and outfits. Decor and outfits are used immediately when bought; food and toys are saved for later and consumed automatically the next time you feed or play. Check the backpack icon to see what you're holding. Once decor is placed or an outfit is worn, drag it around the room to reposition it exactly where you want — your layout is saved automatically.

Sharing this file

Your pet's progress is saved only in this browser, not inside the file itself — if you send this HTML file to someone else and they open it on their own computer, they'll automatically start with a brand-new pet. Reopening it yourself in the same browser will always pick up where you left off, which is why it can look "stuck" on an old session.
